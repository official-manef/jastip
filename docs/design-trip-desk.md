# Trip desk

Reading this as: a waybill-style calculator for Jakarta–Istanbul jastipers, using the existing cream paper and jade palette. ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Color: preserve cream, ink, jade and terracotta; terracotta indicates losses and excess weight.
- Layout: trip identity and backups precede the calculation. The summary shows the effect of edits. Small screens stack inputs and show the summary before long lists.
- Typography: preserve Fraunces headings and Outfit body text from the existing identity. Tabular numerals make prices easier to compare.
- Spacing: group related inputs closely, with larger gaps between baggage, goods and expenses.
- Surfaces: bordered paper sections group editable data; elevation belongs to the summary.
- Motion: native input states and the existing meter transition show changes; reduced-motion disables the animation.
- Assets: reuse the logo, favicon and share card. No new assets or customer claims.
- Theme: retain the paper-style light theme specified by the project.

Target means projected service revenue in IDR. Purchase prices are separate. Payment recognition is not implemented. Users supply actual fees, dates, exchange rates and targets; sample numbers are not market claims.
